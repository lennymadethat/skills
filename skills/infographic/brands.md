# Brand palettes for the house infographic

One row per brand. Measure the values from the product's own CSS; do not guess.

| Brand | Accent | Ink (text on white) | Panel fill | Character | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| (example) a memory product | blue `#0071BC` (red `#ED1C24` sparingly) | navy `#12294A` | soft blue `#EAF3FB` | a librarian | its `app.css` |
| (example) a green reading app | mint `#5FB089` (gold `#D4A84B` sparingly) | near-black `#0D1117` | mint tint `#EAF5EF` | the reader | its `hub.css` |

Rules: the accent is the product's DARK-mode accent moved onto a white ground; ink is the darkest
surface colour of that product, used as text. Fill = accent at ~8% over white. When a brand is
missing here, measure it from its CSS and add a row.

**Colour law:** never orange as an infographic accent, for any brand. Default to blue `#0071BC` on
white with navy text; red `#ED1C24` only as a second primary or for status. Brands with their own
dark accents (mint, sky, cyan) may use them, but never orange.
